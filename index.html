About

A builder who ships, then sells it in.

Ugochukwu — "Yougi" — is a product manager and growth strategist based in Enugu, Nigeria, known for turning early-stage ideas into products with real market pull. He's spent four years across product discovery, GTM positioning, and partnership sourcing, most recently focused on financial technology in African markets.

He's led and built with teams whose products have gone on to be recognized at both national and global stages.

Currently leads Operations at Poolfi, a group financial coordination platform.
