About

The person behind
the product.

I'm Ugochukwu "Yougi" Okwara — a founder, product manager, and GTM strategist operating at the intersection of Web3, African commerce, and consumer trust. I'm currently building TrustScan, a product authentication platform, and Leading Growth for PoolFi, an on-chain group money collection platform for African communities.

Before being a full-time founder, I spent years as a business developer and product lead understanding how markets move, how deals close, and how to translate user insight into product decisions that compound. I bring that muscle to every layer of a startup.

What I Do

Three disciplines.
One operating mode.

01
Product Management
I define the right thing to build ; then build it right.

From discovery to roadmap to shipped feature, I own the full product cycle. I root decisions in user insight, business model constraints, and technical reality not frameworks for their own sake.

02
GTM Strategy
I take products from prototype to paying customer.

I craft the positioning, identify the right beachhead, sequence the launch, and build the outreach infrastructure that turns warm conversations into signed pilots. I've done this from zero with no playbook.

03
Business Development
I build the relationships that become the moat.

BD for early-stage startups is pattern recognition on steroids — knowing which partnerships unlock distribution, which conversations lead to checks, and how to qualify both without wasting anyone's time.
